# Brag Plan: Autonomous AI Agency

## What is this app?
A self-hosted platform that turns one website URL into a working AI operations team — it audits your site, provisions the specialist agents your business needs, and runs them 24×7 on your own hardware, with human approval gates on anything that ships.

## The angle
Proof over hype. This product's own README opens with "Most autonomous agent projects show you a demo video. Here are artifacts instead." So the video leans into that: it does not *claim* the agents work — it shows the one number that proves it. **323 of 906 merged pull requests in this repo were opened by the agent fleet.** Every scene is a real, checkable claim from the product, not a mood board.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark terminal. A single line types out in mono: `paste your website URL_`. The whole premise of the product in five words, before anything else moves.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The audit lands: a health score dial counts up to 74/100 while six pillar tags settle in — Technical · Content · Security · Social · GEO · AIO — with "102 checks · minutes, not weeks."
- The agency assembles: specialist agent chips arrive one by one (SEO, Security, DevOps, Frontend, Backend…) under "35 specialist families," then the plan → execute → verify loop draws across.
- The proof stat: `323 / 906` counts up big — "merged PRs written by the agents themselves."

## Outro / punchline
Wordmark lock-up: **Autonomous AI Agency**, then the line "Self-hosted · MIT · Your servers, your models, your data," then the URL. The last frame is postable on its own.

## User flow worth showing
entry → key action → result: **paste a URL** (Scene 1) → **audit runs, score + 102 checks across 6 pillars** (Scene 2) → **specialists provisioned + plan/execute/verify loop running** (Scene 3). The proof stat (Scene 4) is the payoff that says the flow actually produces work.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film — proof over hype, every claim a real number
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, confident restraint. Motion is smooth (slides, soft fades), never frantic. The numbers do the talking; typography is the design.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, restrained bed with a few precise accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady, clean — the polished pick)
- Music treatment: start at 0, volume ~0.3, gentle fade-out under the final logo
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json; target 1 strong cue for the 323/906 reveal; snap the specialist-chip sequence to the beat grid
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the accent glow on the score dial and the proof number breathe with RMS. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, professional restraint (2-4 cues total)
- Audio-coupled moments: typed hook (soft key ticks), score count-up (rising tick), chip sequence (soft drops), 323/906 payoff (one bell)
- Restraint rule: no aggressive hits, no glitch, nothing stacked; silence between accents is fine

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Paste your URL — 3.2s
Dark terminal frame. `paste your website URL_` types out in IBM Plex Mono, accent-blue caret blinking. Small muted label above: "the entry point is read-only." Holds ~1s after typing completes.
Sequential/interaction: yes — hook line types character by character; caret blinks.
Audio intent: quiet, focused, "we're starting."
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key ticks under the typing.
Music: steady bed enters low.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The audit — 4.8s
A URL chip (`https://yourcompany.com`) sits top-left. A circular health-score dial fills and counts 0 → 74 (/100). Six pillar tags fade up in a row: Technical · Content · Security · Social · GEO · AIO. Caption: "102 checks · minutes, not weeks."
Sequential/interaction: yes — dial arc + number count up together; six tags settle (hold the full row, don't outrun reading).
Audio intent: rising, confident, resolves when the score lands.
Audio-coupled idea: soft rising tick during count-up; one gentle drop when 74 settles.
Music: bed continues.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The agency assembles — 5.0s
Heading "35 specialist families." Agent chips arrive one by one: SEO · Security · DevOps · Frontend · Backend · Data · Growth. Beneath them, a thin pipeline draws: Plan → Execute → Verify, with a small "human approval gate" marker. Caption: "provisioned from one URL. running 24×7."
Sequential/interaction: yes — chips appear one by one (snap to every-other-beat so labels stay readable), then the loop line draws left→right.
Audio intent: assembling, purposeful.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft card/drop per chip; no sound on the loop draw.
Music: bed continues, slight lift.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The proof — 4.0s
Near-black. One huge stat counts up: `323 / 906`. Label below: "merged PRs written by the agents themselves." Small muted line: "not a demo — the public commit history." The accent glow breathes once as it lands.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 323 counts up fast then holds; the "/906" is already present as the denominator.
Audio intent: the payoff. Everything else was setup for this number.
Audio-coupled idea: one restrained bell exactly when 323 lands (the single strong-cue lock).
Music: bed present, ducks slightly for the bell.
Transition mood: clean cut → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Lock-up / outro — 3.0s
Wordmark **Autonomous AI Agency** fades up centered. Line: "Self-hosted · MIT · Your servers, your models, your data." URL beneath: autonomous-ai-agency.strikersam.workers.dev. Accent underline sweeps once.
Sequential/interaction: wordmark → tagline → url, quick staggered fade; underline sweep last.
Audio intent: settle and resolve.
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the bell from Scene 4 ring into this); music fades out.
Music: gentle fade to silence.
Transition mood: hold to end.
